What it does

Small extension. Does the boring part for you.

Snaps exactly

Every post lands right under the site's header. No half posts, no scrolling back.

⌨

Global hotkeys

Next, previous, auto, like, save. They work while a game or another app has focus.

Auto-scroll

Every 1 second to 5 minutes. Hover the page to pause, move the mouse away to go on.

Flips galleries

Goes through every image of a gallery first, then on to the next post.

▶

Waits for videos

Lets a video play through before moving on, redgifs embeds included.

⛶

Full-screen mode

Auto-scroll can open every picture in the site's own viewer, one after another.

♥

Like & save

Upvote or like, save or bookmark the post you're on, with one key.

⤼

Skips the filler

Text posts, ads and "Who to follow" boxes are skipped. Pictures and videos only.

Global hotkeys

Combinations with Ctrl or Alt. They reach your feed even while a game or another app has focus.

Page keys

Any single key, like the arrow keys. They work while the Reddit or X window is focused.

⚠️ Pick global keys you never use elsewhere. A global hotkey stops doing its normal job in every other program and game while the browser runs and GoonScroller is on, even when paused or with Reddit/X closed. Turning GoonScroller off in the extensions page gives the key back. League of Legends blocks global browser hotkeys completely.

Privacy

It runs on your computer. That's it.

  • No tracking, no analytics, no accounts.
  • No servers of ours. Your settings stay in your browser.
  • The only extra request is to Reddit itself, when you press save.
  • Open source under the MIT license: anyone can read every line.

Install

Ready in under a minute.

  1. 1
    Open GoonScroller in the Chrome Web Store and click Add to Chrome.
  2. 2
    A setup page opens. Set the hotkeys to Global so they work in games.
  3. 3
    Open Reddit or X and press Ctrl Shift 2. That's it.
  1. 1
    Download the zip from the latest release and unzip it.
  2. 2
    Open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode (top right).
  3. 3
    Click Load unpacked and pick the unzipped folder.
  4. 4
    A setup page opens. Set the hotkeys to Global and you're done.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. Free and open source, no ads, no premium version.

Does it work on Firefox or my phone?

No. It needs global hotkeys, and only desktop Chromium browsers have those: Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera, Vivaldi.

Which sites?

Reddit (new and old) and X/Twitter. Nothing else, on purpose.

I saved a Reddit post but the Save button didn't change?

Reddit only shows it after a refresh. The post is saved; check your saved posts.

Will the hotkeys mess with my game?

Pick combinations your game doesn't use, like the default Ctrl Shift 1–4, or change them to whatever you like.
